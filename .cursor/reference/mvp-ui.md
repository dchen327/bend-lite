## Routine setup

- Mobile-first white screen with a compact header: close, routine name, reorder/menu.
- Vertically scrolling exercise rows with circular illustrations.
- Exercise name on the left; minus, duration, and plus controls on the right.
- Per-side exercises show a small switch/sides badge over the illustration.
- Fixed bottom actions: outlined Share and full-width blue Start.

## Active player

- Compact header with close, current step count, and menu.
- Large circular exercise illustration with a progress ring.
- Centered exercise name with an information affordance.
- Large muted countdown below the name.
- Bottom transport controls: previous, prominent pause/resume, and next.
- Minimal white, distraction-free presentation with generous spacing.

## Timing

- A per-side duration is the total active time and switches sides halfway.
- Side switches use a 3-second transition.
- Exercises use a 3-second transition.
